How CareLoop works

One message from your team. The rest is automatic.

Your discharge desk sends one WhatsApp when a patient leaves. Everything after runs on its own — from the hospital's own number.

Step 1
Staff logs the discharge — 20 seconds Patient name, phone, doctor, department. Stored internally. Never sent to the patient.
Step 2
CareLoop asks the patient, privately, once they are home A simple 1–5 on WhatsApp, 4 hours after discharge. One tap. Far more people reply than you'd expect.
Step 3
Score routes automatically Every respondent gets a review link. 1 or 2 — your grievance officer also gets an instant alert with the patient's name, department, their words, their number. Your team calls that day.
Step 4
Monthly report, first of every month Patients reached, average score by department, complaints raised, how many resolved within 24 hours. Audit-ready without anyone compiling it.
