FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Do I need to install an app or learn a dashboard?

No. Rebound has no app, no login and no dashboard. Everything, bookings in, reminders out, review alerts, your monthly report, happens inside one WhatsApp chat. If you can use WhatsApp, you already know how to use Rebound.

How do I add a booking?

Forward it, type it, or send a voice note, in plain, everyday language, English or Hinglish. "Priya 98200 12345 haircut kal 4pm" is enough. Rebound writes it down and confirms back within seconds. If anything is unclear, it asks, it never guesses.

Which number do my customers see messages from?

Your own. During setup we create an official WhatsApp Business account in your business name. You'll need one mobile number dedicated to your business WhatsApp, if you don't have one, we help you arrange it on the setup call. A message from "Bloom Salon" gets answered; a message from an unknown number gets ignored.

What happens when someone leaves a bad review?

First, every customer gets a private "how was your visit?" message. If someone wasn't happy, you're told immediately so you can call and make it right. The Google invite still goes to everyone, we never pick and choose, because that's against Google's rules and can get a listing removed. If a poor review does appear, you get a polite, ready-made reply, say APPROVE and it's posted.

What do I need to get started?

Three things: your appointment diary (any format, even photos of a register work), your Google business listing login, and a mobile number dedicated to your business WhatsApp. We handle everything else on one setup call.

Is this only for salons?

Salons and spas, yes, that's deliberate. Both run the same way: one diary, walk-in-adjacent bookings, and a service menu where people return every few weeks. Come-back gaps are set per service, so a haircut nudges at 3 weeks and a facial at 6. Clinics, gyms and physios are on the roadmap, not live, we'd rather do two things properly than five badly.

Won’t people just wait for the free offer instead of booking normally?

It doesn’t work on a schedule, so there’s nothing to wait for. It only goes out after someone’s personal gap has passed and they’re already overdue, someone who books regularly will never see one. There’s a long wait before anyone can receive a second one, and it stops entirely if two are ignored. And honestly: someone who comes back every four months for a ₹400 offer is still coming to you four times a year instead of going elsewhere.

What happens if I go over my plan’s appointment limit?

Nothing stops. Every reminder still goes out, we will never switch off your customer messages over a billing question. You'll get a friendly heads-up at around 80%, and we'll talk about moving you up a plan when it suits you.

How do I know it's actually working?

On the 1st of every month, one message lands in your chat: no-shows dodged, money kept, reviews gained, and how your rating moved against the shop you asked us to watch. Counted against your own numbers from before Rebound, one honest number, not a dashboard.

What if I want to leave?

Cancel anytime. We hand you back all your reviews and your customer list, it's your data, and it leaves with you.
