Cancellation and Refunds

Last updated 20 October 2026

No contract, no lock-in. You can stop whenever you like.

How to cancel

Send us a message at snehalp@gmail.com, or reply to any email or WhatsApp message from us, and say you want to stop. You can also remove us as Manager on your Google page yourself at any time.

Monthly plan

  • After you cancel, we stop at the end of the month you have paid for. You are not charged again.
  • If we miss a review in your first month, that month is free.

Yearly plan

  • If you cancel during the year, write to us. We will refund the full months you have not used.

Refund timing

Refunds go back to the way you paid. They usually take 5 to 7 working days to show.

What happens to your data

You keep every reply and update we wrote. If you ask, we will delete your information (see Privacy).
