How it works

  1. You fill a short formAbout 5 minutes. You paste the link to your Google page, and add us as Manager on it so we can reply for you. You stay the owner, and the form shows you how.
  2. We write the replies and the updatesShort, friendly, in plain English. Each reply is written for that review, so it reads like you took the time.
  3. Unhappy reviews wait for youUnhappy (1 to 3 star) and sensitive reviews come to your email first. Nothing is posted until you tap Post in that email. Happy reviews are answered for you, or you can choose to approve every reply.
  4. You see it is workingOnce a month you get a short report: how many reviews you gained, how you compare with shops near you, and what is missing on your Google page.
