Calling the shops we sent a free sample to

Every shop below got a free 2-page PDF on WhatsApp with ready replies to their Google reviews. Your job: reach the owner, check they saw it, explain the service in 30 seconds, and send the form link. Tick a shop when you are done; your ticks and notes stay saved on this phone or computer.

Before you call

What ReviewSaathi does

  • Replies to every new Google review for the shop, usually within a day.
  • Puts 4 updates a month on their Google page (a service, an offer, timings). The owner OKs them first.
  • Replies to angry or sensitive reviews wait for the owner's approval.
  • A short report every month.

Price: Rs.3,499 a month + GST, or Rs.34,999 a year + GST. Same for every shop. Monthly can stop any time.

Who to ask for, and when

  • Clinics: ask for the doctor by name (shown on each card). Call 2 to 4 pm, or 11 am to 12.
  • Salons: ask for the owner. Call 11 am to 1 pm. Many salons are shut on Tuesday; avoid evenings and weekends.
  • Garages: ask for the owner (malik). Call 10 am to 12 or 3 to 5 pm on weekdays.
  • If the person on the phone is staff, get the owner's name and a good time, and ask them to show the PDF to the owner.

Keep it short

  • 2 to 4 minutes a call. Talk naturally; do not read word for word.
  • No need to remember numbers. The point is simple: we write their review replies so they do not have to.
  • One goal: send the form link. Do not explain everything.
  • Write the outcome on the card straight after the call.

Never say or do

  • Never promise "top of Google" or more customers.
  • Never offer fake or paid reviews.
  • Never ask for a password or OTP.
  • No discounts. The price is the same for everyone.
  • Do not argue. If they say no twice, thank them and stop.

Follow-up rules

  • No answer: try once more after 2 hours, then the next day. After 3 tries, send the "had not seen the PDF" message and stop.
  • "Call later": call exactly at the time they gave.
  • "Will think": one call after 2 days. Then stop.
  • Form sent: if they have not filled it in 2 days, one call to help.
  • Signed up, or a question you cannot answer: tell Snehal the same day.
